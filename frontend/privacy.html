Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

AnyDown ("we", "our") provides a media retrieval and download utility. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Nothing about you. AnyDown has no accounts, no sign-in and no cookies. We do not collect your name, email address, IP-based identity, or any profile information, because there is no account to associate it with.
  • Download Event Metadata: We record operational download events (provider hostname, requested resolution height, and timestamp) for rate limiting and service integrity. We do not store complete user source URLs in event logs.

3. Cookies & Local State

AnyDown sets no cookies and uses no third-party tracking or advertising cookies. Nothing about your visit is stored on your device by this service.

4. Data Retention & Temporary Storage

  • Retrieved media files are temporarily stored in server memory/disk only for the duration required to stream the file to your device. Temporary files and expired jobs are systematically purged every 5 minutes (maximum file lifetime of 30 minutes).
  • No user records exist, so there is nothing further to retain or delete.

5. Your Rights

Because no personal data is collected and no account is created, there is no account to delete and no personal data held about you. If you have a question about this policy, contact the service administrator.

6. Third-Party Services

Media retrieval utilizes the open-source utility yt-dlp communicating with external media content providers chosen by the user. Those providers see the requests this service makes on your behalf, in the same way as any direct client would.

Runs on yt-dlp. For your own content, or content you have the rights to.
