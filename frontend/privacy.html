Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

AnyDown ("we", "our") provides a media retrieval and download utility. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Identity & Profile Information: When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google Subject ID (sub), email address, name, and profile avatar URL provided by Google Identity Services. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Session Information: To keep you securely logged in across requests, we store a cryptographic hash of your session token in our database and set a secure, HttpOnly cookie on your browser.
  • Download Event Metadata: We record operational download events (provider hostname, requested resolution height, authentication status, and timestamp) for rate limiting and service integrity. We do not store complete user source URLs in event logs.

3. Cookies & Local State

We use a single, secure first-party cookie (__Host-anydown_session / anydown_session) solely for maintaining your authenticated session. This cookie is marked HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

4. Data Retention & Temporary Storage

  • Retrieved media files are temporarily stored in server memory/disk only for the duration required to stream the file to your device. Temporary files and expired jobs are systematically purged every 5 minutes (maximum file lifetime of 30 minutes).
  • User account records and session tokens are retained until session expiration or account deletion.

5. Account Deletion & Rights

You can revoke your session at any time by clicking "Sign out". If you wish to delete your user account and all associated records, contact the service administrator or submit a deletion request.

6. Third-Party Services

Our authentication relies on Google Identity Services (Google LLC). Media retrieval utilizes the open-source utility yt-dlp communicating with external media content providers chosen by the user.
